About ReWear

Why a clothing swap, and how it works.

Most wardrobes hold clothes that fit, look good and never get worn. ReWear connects those clothes with people nearby who will wear them.

It started as a college micro-project built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and ES6 JavaScript. Listings load with fetch and async/await, and filters run on array methods.
